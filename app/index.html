    Road to the Platinum

    Missable trophies

    Only one trophy in GTA 6 is reported as possibly missable. Everything else can be earned in free roam after the story, and there is no mission replay — so the save advice below matters.

    Save discipline (no mission replay)

    1. Keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter — 15 manual slots, 1 autosave slot. Ten saves cover the five chapters; rotate the other five for free-roam cleanup.
    2. Save in a fresh slot before the final mission, and keep your Chapter 1 save until Positive Mental Attitude pops.
    3. Never turn cheats on during a trophy run: cheats disable trophies and saving.
    4. Optional missions and quest givers that vanish for a while come back later — only the Chapter 1 meditation is gone for good.
